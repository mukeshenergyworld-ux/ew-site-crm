EW Visit

Log a site visit from the site

Who this is for: sales executives. One screen, one job — log the visit while you are still standing at the site.

Signing in

Your mobile number and a PIN. This app does not use the CRM name and PIN — it uses your number.

First time in, your PIN is the last four digits of your own number. Change it straight away with Change PIN at the bottom. If you forget it, the office resets it — the app cannot.

Logging a visit

  1. Who did you go to — type a few letters and tap the name.
  2. What is he — tap the chip, if it is not already filled in.
  3. Site — tap a site he already has, or + A new site.
  4. Why you went — New pitch, Follow-up, Payment, Delivery check, Complaint or Courtesy.
  5. What stage the site is at — excavation, slab, concealed rough-in, wall and tiling, fixture fitting, systems and handover. This is the one that decides what to pitch next, so it is worth setting properly.
  6. What happened — Interested, Asked for a quote, Got the order, Later, Not now or Just met.
  7. Next step and By when, and a Note if there is anything to say.
  8. Save the visit.

Press it once. With no signal it waits on the phone and goes up on its own.

The location

The app looks for the location by itself. If you refuse it, or it cannot find one, the visit still saves — it says so: no location, never mind.

What it will not do

  • It will not save without a name. That is the one thing a visit cannot be saved without.
  • It cannot register a new client or partner. He goes on the register in the CRM first, then he appears here.
  • It will not let you sign out while a visit is still on the phone. Send it first.
  • No money, no challan, no editing, no deleting. It records visits and that is all it does.

The badge that says something is waiting

not sent beside the title is the count still on the phone. It clears itself when there is signal. The ↻ arrow pulls the latest register and offers a new version if one is out.
