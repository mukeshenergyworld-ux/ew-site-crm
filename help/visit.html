EW Visit & Quote - how to use

For the sales executives in the field: mark every visit, log site visits, and make and send quotes. Full detail is in the CRM, at the office.

Three rules

  • Same name and PIN as the CRM. Open /visit/ and add it to the home screen.
  • A site belongs to whoever entered it first. You can still log your visit on someone else’s site — it shows as a later visit.
  • No signal? Save anyway. The phone keeps it and sends it when the signal is back. The line at the bottom says how many are waiting.

My day

The first screen. + Mark a visit and + New quote, then today’s visits, quotes to follow up (sent or negotiating, silent 5 days), quotes made but not sent, follow-ups due (with Call), and your sites not visited for 30 days.

Mark a visit

  1. Site: search the site (name, client, mobile, area or partner), or Sites near me. Open it and tap Log a visit here: whom you met, what happened, the stage now. Your location goes with it.
  2. Not on the book? + New site. If the mobile or name is already there you are shown who entered it — open that site instead.
  3. Plumber, Architect, Showroom: pick the name (or type a new one with his mobile), write a line, Save the visit. A new plumber or architect with a mobile is added as a partner.

Make a quote

  1. The client (only your own). New? + A new client — name and 10-digit mobile.
  2. Pick a brand, find the products, Add, set the quantity with − and +.
  3. Discount per brand — his preset is filled in. Your incentive moves with it.
  4. Check and Save the quote. If another executive already quoted him the same products you are warned: one client, one price.
  5. WhatsApp the PDF sends the same PDF as the CRM and marks the quote Sent. Download PDF keeps a copy. Every new quote also goes to the office quote group.
  6. Later: Negotiating, Won or Lost (with the reason).
