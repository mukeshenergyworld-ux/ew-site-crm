Taking over the hisab

For accounts. Your first two weeks, one job at a time.

Who this is for: whoever holds the accounts role. Until now the owner has done every one of these himself — all 123 deliveries on the book were passed by him. You are taking one job off him, not learning the whole app. The full CRM guide is here when you want it.

What you are taking over

One loop, every day, and it is the same four steps each time. A delivery goes out. The paper comes back signed. You check the paper against the delivery. You put it into the customer’s hisab, so it becomes money he owes.

Until that last step happens, the delivery is not on his account and nobody is chasing it. That is the whole value of this job: material that went out three weeks ago and was never put into hisab is money nobody is asking for.

Your day, in order

  1. Open the CRM and go to Challans. Anything with no receipt on it is a delivery whose paper has not come back — ring the site or the driver and get it photographed.
  2. When the paper is on the card, look at it. Tap the picture to open the full document. Check the challan number on the paper is the one on the card, and that it is signed.
  3. If the photograph is the wrong page, tap Change and put the right one on. The old one is kept — nothing is ever deleted here.
  4. If the material has been delivered but the card does not say so, tap Mark as received first. Hisab will not take a delivery that is not received.
  5. Now tap the green ADD TO HISAB button on the card.
  6. Read the screen. The lines, the rates, the discount, the total. Tap Open the signed receipt once more if you want to compare.
  7. Tap Add to HISAB. That is it — it is on his account from that moment.

Then go to Client dues and Payments, and work the chase list. That part is the EW Payment guide.

Three things it will refuse, and why

It saysWhat to do
Mark it as received firstThe material is not booked in. Find out whether it actually reached the site, and mark it. A delivery nobody has confirmed must not become a bill.
There is no signed receipt — say in one line whyIt does not stop you. Type the reason in one line — “owner delivered himself”, “paper lost, site confirmed on phone”. It is kept with the stamp for good, because in six months a delivery with no paper and no reason cannot be told apart from a mistake.
Ask the owner for the discount on …That brand has never been priced for this client. Only the owner sets a discount. Ask him for the percent, or that there is none; he puts it on the client’s card, and the delivery then goes straight in.

What you will not see, and it is not a fault

The screen does not show you what any partner or executive earns on a delivery, and it has no box for setting a discount or taking money off. That is deliberate. Those are the owner’s, and they stay on his screen only.

It changes nothing about your job. When you press Add to HISAB, everyone who should earn on that delivery is recorded exactly as the owner would have recorded them. You do not have to think about it and you must not be asked to.

The one to be careful with. A delivery goes into hisab once. If you are not sure whether it should — the paper looks wrong, the quantity does not match, the customer is disputing — do not stamp it. Leave it and ask. An unstamped delivery is a job still to do; a wrongly stamped one is a wrong bill that somebody has to unpick.

Two weeks from now

You should be able to open Challans in the morning and find nothing older than a day or two waiting for paper, and nothing received sitting outside hisab. When that is true, the owner stops looking at this screen at all.

True of every app here
