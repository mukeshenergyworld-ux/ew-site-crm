Signing in and finding your way

Who: Everyone.

The CRM opens in the phone's browser - nothing to install. The same name and PIN open every staff app.

Sign in

  1. Open the CRM link the office sent you. Type Your name exactly as the office wrote it, and Your PIN.
  2. Tap Sign in. The phone remembers you - next time it opens straight on your work.
Your PIN is yours. Nobody else types it and nobody should ever ask you for it. Forgot it? Ask the owner - he sets a new one on Team PINs.

The top of every screen

  1. Your name and your role are at the top. ↻ Refresh pulls the latest book from the office. Account shows who is signed in, Face ID / fingerprint and Sign out.
  2. The search box finds anything - a client, a quote number, a challan, a product, a plumber. Type three letters.

The menu: eight headings, each opens its screens

  1. The first row is the headings: Agent, HISAB, Deliveries, Clients, Service, Products, Team, Today. Tap one.
  2. The row under it shows that heading's screens. The dark one is where you are. Tap another to move.
  3. Every screen has a ? How to use button under the menu. It opens this guide at that screen's own section.
You only see the headings and screens your role needs. If a screen in this guide is missing on your phone, it belongs to another role - that is on purpose.

Agent

Agent - your to-do list

Where: Agent → Agent

Who: Everyone. Each person sees the work on his own book.

The Agent reads the whole book every time and ranks what to do today: sites where a brand's window is closing, money to collect, quotes waiting. It never sends anything by itself.

Work down today's list

  1. Open Agent. The four boxes count Do today, This week, Keep an eye on and Snoozed / done. Start with Do today.
  2. The chips under it narrow the list: windows closing, money to collect, key stage, quotes waiting.
  3. One card is one site. It lists every brand whose window is closing there - ✓ means already quoted. Call client and Call plumber dial straight from the card.
  4. One message, all 4 writes one WhatsApp message for the whole site. Snooze 7d hides the card for a week. Done clears it.

Owner: send the list to the team

  1. Send today’s list to the team (Telegram) posts the list to the team group. Remind executives sends each executive his own sites.

Clients

Leads - add a new customer

Where: Clients → Leads

Who: Sales executives, the owner and accounts.

Every new person starts here as a lead. He becomes a client by himself the day he wins a brand or takes a delivery - nobody sets that by hand.

Add a new lead

  1. Open Clients → Leads and tap + New lead.
  2. Fill Client name, District and Area / colony (both needed), Mobile, and the Architect and Plumber on his site. The partners you name here earn on every quote and challan later.
  3. Tap the construction stage of his site - one tap. The stage decides what to pitch there and when. Don't know? Tap Don’t know yet.
  4. Tap Save client without stage or, after picking a stage, the save button at the bottom. The lead is on your book at once.
Only the owner may set an opening balance, a credit limit or move a lead to another executive. Those boxes stay locked for everyone else.

See who you have quoted

  1. The Leads screen lists everyone you have quoted, oldest quote first - that is the one going cold. The chips filter Waiting to close, Won and Lost.
  2. Open client opens his full page: sites, quotes, challans, payments and his HISAB.

Quotes - make and send a quotation

Where: Clients → Quotes

Who: Sales executives and the owner.

A quote is built in five steps: Client, Brand, Products, Discount, Review. The discount already agreed for that client and brand fills itself in.

Make a new quote

  1. Tap + New quote.
  2. 1. Client - tap the district, type the first letters of his name, tap Continue. Check the site stage shown, then Continue to the products.
  3. 2. Brand - tap the brand. The % beside it is the discount already set for this client.
  4. 3. Products - tap a category, then + to add quantity. Add another brand on the same quote the same way. + Bathroom files the next items under a room (optional).
  5. 4. Discount - each brand has one box. Type the % and tap Apply brand discounts. ▼ Change discount product-wise sets it line by line.
  6. 5. Review - check every line and the total. Tick Show 18% GST on the quote PDF if he wants GST printed. Tap Save quote.
The Margin box on the review is the owner's eyes only - it never prints and never shows to an executive.
By client (the list's usual view) puts every quote under the client's name. A quote that was revised shows Revision 1, Revision 2, Revision 3, the newest marked latest. List shows the plain sheet, sortable by any heading.

Send it, then close it

  1. In the list, PDF makes the quotation to send on WhatsApp. A quote left on Draft has not reached the customer - send it, and it moves to Sent.
  2. When he decides, tap Won or Lost. Every quote should end one way or the other - an open quote is chased by the Follow-ups radar until it does.

Follow-ups - quotes going cold

Where: Clients → Follow-ups

Who: Sales executives and the owner.

The radar lists every quote marked Sent or Negotiating with no news for 5 days or more. The red number on the chip is how many.

Chase a quote

  1. Each card is one quote: client, brand, amount, how many days quiet. Download PDF or Proposal to send it again, Telegram to post it.
  2. Heard back? Tap Won or Lost. Not yet? Snooze hides it until the date you choose.

Set yourself a reminder

  1. Scroll down to the reminders and tap + New follow-up: pick the client, the date and a note. Mark done when it is done.

Deliveries

Challans - from godown to signed receipt

Where: Deliveries → Challans

Who: Godown makes it, accounts (or the owner) passes it, the driver brings back the signed paper. Sales executives can see their clients' challans.

A challan moves through the same steps every time. The screen shows how many are waiting at each step.

The life of a challan

Made (godown) → Passed with a PIN (accounts / owner) → Dispatched (left the godown) → Received (signed paper back) → Finalised into the client's HISAB → Billed.

  1. The counters at the top show how many wait at each step. Tap one to see just those.

Make a challan (usually done in the Challan app)

  1. Tap + New challan. The godown normally uses the EW Challan app for this - 📦 Open the Challan app opens it.
  2. Pick the Client - only a registered client can be picked, so his preset discount always applies. Not there? + Register new.
  3. Tap the brand, then the category, then + for the quantity - the same way as a quote.
  4. Pick the driver (his mobile and vehicle fill in), the freight, and tap Create challan.

Pass it (accounts / owner)

  1. When challans wait, a red box says so on every screen. Tap Pass them now →.
  2. Check the challan and tap Pass & Dispatch.
  3. Type your own PIN once and tap Dispatch it. Until you close the app, every next challan is one tap.
The PIN is the stamp. Never pass a challan on somebody else's PIN, and never give yours.
A mistake? In the EW Challan app, before it leaves the godown, the godown taps Edit or Cancel challan (with a reason) - nothing is deleted, the owner can bring it back. After it has left, the godown taps Ask to cancel; the request waits for the owner here in the CRM and he decides. The godown does not pass challans.

The signed receipt comes back

  1. Open Receipts pending. Each delivery still waiting for its paper is listed, newest day first.
  2. Attach receipt takes a photo of the signed challan. Mark as received says the goods reached the site.
Until the signed paper is in, the delivery is not on the client's account, the driver's trip does not count, and nobody earns on it. Chase the paper.

Material returns

Where: Deliveries → Material returns

Who: Godown and accounts.

Material coming back from a site. The original challan is never changed - a return is its own record, so what went out and what came back can be checked against each other.

Register a return

  3. As it moves: Picked up when the driver collects it, Received at godown when it is counted in, then Finalise to credit the client.

HISAB

HISAB - a client's account

Where: HISAB → HISAB

Who: The owner and accounts.

One client's whole account: every delivery, return, payment and the balance - and from here you finalise deliveries, take payments and download his statement.

Open a client's HISAB

  1. Type the client's name in the box and tap Show - or tap a client in the list below.
  2. His account opens. ⇩ PDF is the statement to send him; ⇩ Excel the same as a sheet.

Finalise a delivery onto his account

  1. A received delivery shows Finalise. Tap it: this puts the amount on his statement and settles any further discount.
The red box deliveries not on any statement yet lists every delivery that is signed for but not finalised - oldest first. Clear it every day.

Take a payment

  1. In his HISAB tap + Payment (or + Advance for money paid ahead).

HISAB

Payments - who owes, and money in

Where: HISAB → Payments

Who: The owner and accounts. Sales executives see their own clients.

Everyone who owes, oldest money first, and the place to record money as it comes in.

Chase the money

  1. The Collection radar lists who owes, oldest money first. Sort it or filter by age and executive.
  2. Remind sends him a polite WhatsApp with his ledger. → Rohit hands the reminder to his executive.

Record a payment

  1. Tap ₹ In on his line.
  2. Choose Payment received, Advance received or Refund. Type the Amount received, the date, the Mode (Cash, Bank transfer, Cheque, UPI) and the cheque or UTR number. Tap Save payment.
A payment settles his oldest bill first. Paid more than he owes? It is held as credit and comes off his next delivery.

Service - machines, visits and AMC

Where: Service → Service

Who: The service engineers, accounts and the owner.

Every softener, filter, pump and purifier we have put in, when each is next due, and every visit made to it.

Certify a new install

  1. To certify lists delivered machines that still need commissioning on site. At the site, tap Certify install.
  2. Type the commissioning date, pick the engineer and read the serial number off each machine. Tap Certify & generate - the certificate and warranty card are made.

Log a service visit

  1. Find the client in the list and tap Log visit on his machine.
  2. Pick the Type of visit, the visit charge, salt bags and spares used, and Collected now. Tap Save visit.

Add a machine that was put in before the app

  1. Tap + New installation, pick the customer, the brand and product, dates, warranty, and whether it needs periodic service. Periodic service = Yes is what makes the reminders come.
