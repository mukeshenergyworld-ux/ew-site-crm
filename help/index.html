How to use the apps

One page for each. Written for the phone, and made to print.

Every staff app uses the same name and PIN — the one from the Team CRM. There is only ever one. The two apps that do not are EW Visit and EW Saathi, which use a mobile number instead.

If an app turns you away at the door, your role is wrong, not your PIN. Only the owner changes a role, in the team sheet.

Three things that are true of every app

  • Nothing is ever deleted. Not a challan, not a payment, not a partner. Things are blocked, marked, or reversed with a counter-entry — and the record stays.
  • With no signal, save anyway. The staff apps write to the phone first and go up by themselves. A banner counts what is waiting. Nothing typed is lost.
  • If an app cannot work something out, it says so. A blank where a number should be means it does not know. That is information, not a fault.

Your PIN

It is yours. Do not share it, do not write it on the phone, and do not type it for somebody else. Passing a challan asks for it every time on purpose — that is what makes the stamp mean something.
