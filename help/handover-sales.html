Working your own quotes

For the sales executives. Your first two weeks.

Who this is for: whoever holds the sales role. Nothing here is locked — you can already do all of it today. The full CRM guide is here; this page is only the part that is yours.

The one habit this is all about

A quote is not finished when you send it. It is finished when you say what happened to it.

On 23 August the book held 58 quotes still open, the oldest 39 days, and not one quote had ever been marked lost. Fourteen of them belonged to customers who had already taken material since. So nobody could tell a live job from one already won and one already gone.

Closing a quote costs three taps. Not closing it costs the whole list its meaning.

Your day, in order

  1. Open The brief first. It is the short version of what is yours today.
  2. Go to Follow-ups. Anything due today, do it today — a call, a visit, a price. Then set the next one before you close the card, every time. A follow-up with no next date is a customer nobody will ring again.
  3. Go to Quotes. Work down from the oldest. For each one, say what happened: Won if he has taken it, Later if he has approved it but will install after some time, Lost if it has gone elsewhere.
  4. Where you quoted two brands, answer for each brand separately — won on one and later on the other is the normal case, not a special one. Give the come-back date on a Later and the app will raise it again on the day.
  5. On site, use EW Visit on your phone — see the visit guide. Photograph the site, and set the construction stage while you are standing in it.
  6. Set the stage on the Pitch board. What you can sell a site depends entirely on what stage it is at, and the board tells you what to pitch next.

Marking a quote lost is not an admission

It is information, and it is worth as much as a win. Win/Loss only means something once the losses are in it. A brand that keeps losing on price is something the owner can act on — but only if he can see it.

Nothing is deleted when you mark a quote lost. It stays on the customer’s card, with the reason, for good.

In EW Payment your header says view only. You can see who owes what, ring him and remind him — chasing is your job. Writing the money into the book is the office’s.

If a price is not right for a customer, do not work around it. Ask the owner to set the discount on the client’s card. A price agreed on site and never written down is a bill somebody has to argue about later, and it will not be you.

Two weeks from now

Every quote older than a month should carry an answer. Every customer you are working should have a next follow-up date on him. Every site you have visited should have its stage set. When that is true, the list in front of you is real, and Monthly card is a fair account of your month.
