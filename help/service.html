Three rules

  • Nothing is ever deleted. A wrong visit is corrected, and the record stays.
  • No signal? Save anyway. The phone keeps the visit and sends it when the line is back.
  • Always bring the signed receipt. It is the only proof of the visit and the bill.

Today

Today - what is due

Who: The engineers.

The counts at the top - Overdue, Due this week, Under AMC, Machines - and below them every machine waiting for a visit, overdue first.

Do a service visit

  1. Tap the machine you are going to.
  2. Its card shows the address, mobile, last service, next due and the AMC - what is covered and what is charged. Call or WhatsApp the customer from here. At the site tap Record a visit.
  3. Tap what was done - Service, Salt refill, Repair, Installation. Fill salt bags, parts used and the charge. To collect works itself out.
  4. Take the photos: the signed service receipt, the salt bags signed for, the fault, the work done.
  5. Tap Save the visit.
The signed service receipt is what settles any argument about a bill later. Never leave a site without it.
