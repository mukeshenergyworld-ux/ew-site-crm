EW Service

What is due today, the machine's history, and what to collect

Who this is for: the service engineers, the owner, and accounts. It is the app you carry to the customer’s house — what is due today, what the machine has had done to it before, and what to collect. Accounts opens it to read the money, not to record a visit, and the app says so if he tries.

Signing in

Your name and PIN — the same as the Team CRM — then Sign in. If it turns you away, ask the owner to tick service against your name in the team sheet. Nothing else controls it.

The five tabs

MachinesEvery machine on the book: the customer, the model, when it was last serviced and when it is next due.
Labour jobPlumbing work with no machine behind it.
My workThe visits you have done.
AccountThe money side, month by month. An engineer sees his own work — what he charged, what he collected, what is still pending on his visits. The owner and accounts see the whole firm.
A machine with no service date set is counted and shown separately — it is not called due and it is not called fine. The app does not pretend to know. The office sets the date.

Doing a visit

  1. Today → tap the machine.
  2. Tap what was done: Service, Salt refill, Repair or Installation.
  3. Check the date.
  4. On a salt refill, fill Salt bags and Salt amount. The rate from the price list is shown underneath — charge the actual.
  5. Tap + Add a part for any spare fitted, and set the quantity.
  6. Check Visit / labour charge. On a machine with no contract it already has the standard visit charge in it. Change it if this one was different.
  7. Read the bill down the screen. Every line says whether it is to collect or covered.
  8. Type what you actually took into Taken now, then Save the visit.

Leave Taken now blank if the customer is paying the office later. Whatever you type there is money you are carrying.

A repair on a live contract is free — the AMC covers the visit. An installation is always charged: the sale of the machine is not its upkeep.

Three things worth knowing

  • The contract starts after the machine is in. A visit dated before the installation date is not covered by it, however long the contract runs.
  • A contract with no end date on it is honoured, not refused. The office set it and did not type the end. Arguing with a customer at his door is the wrong way round.
  • An old word in the contract cell is read as AMC without spares — the visit free, the parts charged. If a card shows a contract kind that looks wrong, tell the office. It is one tap for them to fix.
When a visit is fully covered the screen says so and offers no money box at all. You are never put in the position of asking a customer for money he has already paid.

Whose money you can see

An engineer sees his own work and nothing else — his visits, what is pending on them, what he collected. That is what the Account tab adds up for him.

The owner and accounts see the whole firm — every engineer’s visits, the takings since the app opened, and what the AMCs gave away.

It is the same screen. It just adds up different rows depending on who is holding the phone.

The rates

The standard visit charge and the salt rate are not built into the app. They are two rows in the Spares price list in the CRM, so the office changes them and every phone follows.

CodeWhat
VISITThe standard visit charge
SALTSalt, per kg

If those rows have not been added, the app suggests nothing and you type every figure yourself. It never guesses a rate.

A labour job

Plumbing work with no machine behind it. Labour job tab, pick the customer and the area, put the charge in, save. There is no AMC on a labour job — there is no machine for a contract to be about.

What the app will not do

  • Nothing is ever deleted. A visit is a row added; a mistake is corrected by the office, not rubbed out here.
  • It invents no next-service date when no cycle is recorded. It moves the last-service date and leaves the next one alone.
  • A background refresh will not happen while you have a form open, so it cannot take your typing away.

When there is no signal

Save anyway. The visit is written to the phone first and goes up by itself. The banner counts what is waiting; Try now pushes it.

The visit is always written before the machine’s due date is moved. If only one of the two makes it, the work is recorded and only the due date is stale — never the other way round.
