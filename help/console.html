EW Console

The back office — broadcasts, sign-ins, versions

Who this is for: the owner, and nobody else. The console is where partners are given a sign-in, where broadcasts go out, and where a new version is pushed to everybody’s phone.

Signing in

Mobile number and PIN. It refuses anyone who is not the owner. The owner’s own PIN cannot be reset from inside the console — that is on purpose.

Sending a broadcast

  1. Broadcast tab. Fill the heading and the body, and the brand, valid-till date and photo link if there are any. Choose who it goes to.
  2. Tap Draft save karein. This saves it. It does not send it. Nobody sees anything yet.
  3. Find it in the list underneath and tap Bhejein.
  4. Read the warning — it will appear on every partner’s phone at once — and tap Haan, bhej dein.

Band karein ends a live broadcast. It is not deleted: the record stays exactly where it is. Phir se bhejein sends an old one again, and warns you if the date on it has already passed.

Sign-ins

The Log tab lists everyone who can sign in, and flags the ones who never have, the ones still on their first PIN, and the ones on an old build.

  • Bulayein sends a man his invite on WhatsApp.
  • PIN reset clears his PIN and warns you first — the one he chose is gone for good.
  • Band karein stops a man signing in. His record, his points and his hisaab all stay exactly as they are. Nothing is deleted.
  • Sign in banayein adds an executive or an owner. A partner cannot be added here — he comes from the Associates sheet.

Pushing a new version

The Update tab shows which version each app is on, and which build each phone is actually running. Pick the app, write a one-line note, and send.

A forced update cannot be taken back from here. If you tick Zaroori update, the old version stops working for everybody. Send an ordinary update unless you have a real reason.
