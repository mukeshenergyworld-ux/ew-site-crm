EW Challan - how to use

Material out, material back, and the signed paper - step by step.

The life of a challan

1MakeGodown - saved as a draft 2Pass & dispatchAccounts, with a PIN 3Signed receiptPhoto of the paper 4Onto the hisabChecked and added 5Driver paidOnly for proved trips

Three rules

  • Nothing is ever deleted. A wrong challan is cancelled, and the record stays.
  • No signal? Save anyway. The phone keeps it and sends it when the line is back.
  • No signed paper, no delivery. Always bring the receipt back and attach it the same day.

Signing in and the bottom bar

Who: The godown, accounts and the owner.

The same name and PIN as the CRM. The bar at the bottom holds the screens: Challan, Return, Approve, Receipts, Freight - and for the owner Check & add and Scorecard.

Sign in

  1. Type your name and your PIN, then tap Sign in.

Move between screens

  1. Tap a screen in the bottom bar. A number on it is how many are waiting there. The last one, CRM, opens the CRM.
  2. ↻ pulls the latest book. ☰ shows who is signed in, Face ID / fingerprint and Sign out. ? opens this guide at the screen you are on.

Challan

Make a challan

Who: The godown (and accounts or the owner when needed).

Material going out to a site. It saves as a draft; it counts against the client only after accounts passes it.

Make a new challan

  1. On Challan, tap + New challan.
  2. Type a few letters of the client's name, firm, area or number, and tap him in the list.
  3. Check the box Before you load: what he already has unbilled and his credit terms. Pick the Site, or + A new site.
  4. Tap + Add material.
  5. Tap the brand, then the category, then + to set each quantity. There is no separate Add button - a quantity puts it on. Tap Done.
  6. Type the Manual Challan No from the paper book, the freight, and pick the Driver. Tap Create the challan.
Something not in the price list? In the material picker use + Item not in the price list or 🔧 Job work / Labour charges.

Find a challan

  1. The chips filter the log: All, Approval pending, Receipt pending, Mine. The arrows move a day at a time. Tap a number to open it.

Approve

Approve - pass and dispatch

Who: Accounts and the owner. The godown does not pass challans.
Godown, a mistake? Before dispatch, open the challan and tap Edit or Cancel challan and pick a reason - nothing is deleted, the owner can bring it back. After dispatch, tap Ask to cancel: the owner reviews it in the CRM and decides.

Only a passed challan counts against the client's account. Passing adds a stamp - it does not change what the godown wrote.

Pass one

  1. Open Approve. Read the challan (View details, Challan PDF), then tap Pass & Dispatch and type your PIN.

Pass several at once

  1. Tick the ones you have read (nothing is ticked for you), or Tick all 4. Then tap Pass & dispatch at the bottom of the ticked list.
Your PIN is asked once; until you close the app, the next one is one tap. Never type someone else's PIN.

Receipts

Receipts - the signed paper

Who: Whoever gets the signed challan back - godown or accounts.

A delivery with no signed paper cannot be proved later. Photograph the signed challan and it stays on that record for good.

Attach a signed receipt

  1. Open Receipts. Find the challan (they are grouped by executive) and tap Attach receipt.
  2. Take a clear photo of the whole signed paper and confirm. The number on Receipts goes down by one.
Until the receipt is in: the money is not on the client's account, the driver's trip is not paid, and nobody earns on it.

Return

Return - material coming back

Who: The godown.

Goods coming back off a site. A return carries its own signed goods-in receipt, just like a delivery.

Register a return

  1. Open Return and tap + Register a return.
  2. Find the client the same way as a challan. Tap + Add material for what is coming back, pick the driver who brought it, and tap Register the return.
  3. When the goods are in the godown, tap Attach goods-in receipt on the return and photograph the signed paper.

Freight

Freight - paying the drivers

Who: Accounts and the owner.

Each driver's trips. A trip is ready to settle once its signed receipt is in; until then it is held.

Pay a driver

  1. Each driver shows what is ready, what is held and what is not yet dispatched. Tap his name for every trip; tap Record a payment when you pay him.

Check & add

Check & add - onto the hisab

Who: The owner.

Open the signed paper, check it against the challan, and add the delivery to the customer's hisab. The list shows what is waiting for your eye; the rest will come once their paper is in.

Scorecard

Client scorecard

Who: The owner (and whoever he allows).

Every buying client graded A to D on buying, paying and returns. Tap a grade to filter, tap a client for his card.

Read it

  1. Tap Needs attention (C, D) to see who to worry about first.
