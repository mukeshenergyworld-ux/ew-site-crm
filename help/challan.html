EW Challan

Material out, material back, and the signed receipt

Who this is for: the godown, accounts and the owner. Nobody else can open it. If you sign in and it turns you away, your role in the team sheet is wrong — only the owner can change it.

Signing in

Type Your name and Your PIN, then tap Sign in. It is the same name and PIN as the Team CRM. There is no separate password for this app.

If you have turned on Face ID or fingerprint on that phone, tap Unlock with Face ID / fingerprint instead. That is only a quicker way of typing the same PIN — it is not a second account.

The five tabs along the bottom

TabWhat it holds
ChallanThe delivery book. Everything sent out in the last 60 days, newest first, grouped under the sales executive whose client it is.
ReturnMaterial coming back in.
ApproveChallans waiting to be passed. The red number is how many.
ReceiptsChallans with nothing signed against them yet.
CRMNot a screen — it opens the Team CRM in another tab.

The search box at the top searches the whole book, not just the last 60 days. Type two letters of a client, a challan number or a book number.

Writing a challan — the everyday job

  1. Tap + New challan.
  2. Find the client. Either type two letters into the search box, or walk down 1 · District → 2 · Area in → his name.
  3. Tap the site. If there is no site, tap None; if it is a new one, tap + A new site.
  4. If you are copying from the paper book, type the number into Manual Challan No.
  5. Tap + Add material. Walk Brand → Category → Product, or use the search box inside that sheet. Setting a quantity adds the line — there is no separate Add button. Tap Done when the load is on the screen.
  6. Fill Freight and Freight borne by if there is any, and pick the Driver.
  7. Tap Create the challan.

The challan is now a Draft. It has a number, and it is not passed. Material must not leave on it yet.

Before you load. The client card shows a line in orange if he is past his credit limit, or if a challan has been sitting unbilled longer than his terms. It is worth reading. That line is worked out only from challans — it does not know what he has actually paid, so ring the office rather than argue with it.

Passing a challan

Only accounts and the owner can pass one. Open the Approve tab and tap Pass it.

It asks for your PIN every single time. That is on purpose. The message says it plainly: this releases material, it is not a formality. Tap Cancel and nothing happens.

Passing a challan sends it to the Telegram group by itself. If Telegram does not take it you will see so, and you can use Send to Telegram on the strip.

A challan still on the phone cannot be passed. If it says Held on this phone, it has not reached the sheet yet. Wait for signal, or tap Try now in the banner at the bottom. Approving is never queued — that would mean keeping your PIN on the phone, and it is not kept anywhere.

The signed receipt

Once a challan is dispatched, Attach receipt appears. Photograph the signed copy and attach it there.

This is done by the office, not the godown. The godown sees a note saying so instead of the button. It is deliberate: the signed proof is not filed by the man it would clear.

The Receipts tab is the list of everything still missing one. Work it down.

Material coming back

  1. Return tab → + Register a return.
  2. Pick the client, then say which delivery it is against — or Not sure if nobody knows.
  3. Tap the reason: Extra material, Wrong size, Damaged, Not required, Wrong item sent or Something else.
  4. Add the material and the driver, then Register the return.
  5. When the goods are in, attach Attach goods-in receipt.

What the app will not let you do

  • You cannot write a challan for a man who is not a client. He has to be registered in the CRM first, so his discount is set before his first delivery ever goes out.
  • You cannot type a client, a site or a driver freely. They are picked from the register. A new driver can be added here; a new client cannot.
  • Only the owner sets a challan discount. Everyone else sees the box but cannot type in it.
  • Nothing is deleted. Passing adds a stamp; it does not change what the godown wrote. Editing a passed challan sends it back to Draft, and it must be passed again — approval cannot carry over to changed contents.
  • The printed PDF of an unpassed challan carries its own warning across it: NOT PASSED YET.

When it says something is held on this phone

Nothing is lost. The banner above the tabs counts what is waiting and it goes up by itself when there is signal. Try now pushes it immediately.

The line under the title tells you how old the copy on that phone is — updated just now, from YESTERDAY, or never pulled onto this phone. If it looks stale, tap the ↻ arrow.

The refresh arrow will refuse while a form is open, because refreshing would throw away what you have typed. Finish or close it first.
