EW Payment

Who to ring today, and the money as it comes in

Who this is for: the owner, accounts, and sales executives. Accounts and the owner may record money. A sales executive can see and ring and remind, but cannot record. That is deliberate — chasing is his job, writing money into the book is the office’s. His header says view only so there is no doubt.

Signing in

Your name and Your PIN — the same ones as the Team CRM — then Sign in. Face ID or fingerprint, if you turned it on, replays the same PIN.

An executive only sees the clients that are his. Accounts and the owner see everyone.

The three tabs

TabWhat it holds
Today's callsThe day’s list. Who promised today, whose promised day has already gone, and then the worst of the rest — most money, longest overdue.
EveryoneThe full chase list, hardest first, with the totals broken down by executive. Tap a name to work only his list.
Old bookBalances carried over from the old paper ledgers, with where each one has got to: figure sent, he agrees, he disputes, no answer yet.

The search box works across the whole book. Anything under ₹500 is not shown — it is not worth a phone call.

Recording a payment

  1. Find the client and tap his card to open it.
  2. Tap Payment received.
  3. Type the Amount received, or tap one of the quick chips (Full, Overdue only, Old book).
  4. Set Date the money came in. It cannot be a future date.
  5. Pick How — bank transfer, cheque, cash, UPI or adjustment — and put the cheque number or UTR in Reference (cheque no, UTR — optional) if there is one.
  6. Tap Record it.
If the same client, same date and same amount is already on the book, it stops you. Two people recording the same cheque is the commonest way money goes wrong here. If it really is a second, separate payment, the button changes and you tap it again to say so.

Once it is recorded the sheet shows the balance now. If the client has a mobile number on file you can then tap Send the receipt on WhatsApp.

Every message is shown to you before it goes

WhatsApp reminder, Send his hisab, the receipt and the old-book figure all stop at a check screen first, showing exactly who it is going to and exactly what it says. The reason is on the screen: a message to a customer cannot be called back.

WhatsApp then opens with the message ready. You still press send inside WhatsApp. The app never sends anything on its own.

Download hisab just downloads the statement PDF — nothing goes to anybody.

Logging a call

Log the call → How did it go? → pick the answer, set the date, and Save the call.

Only Promised a date and Part payment agreed count against him as a promise. Asking to be rung back is a reminder, not a broken word — the app keeps those apart on purpose, so “promise broken” means something.

A promised date cannot be put in the past.

The old book

These are the paper balances. The job on each one is: send him the figure, then record what he said.

  1. Send him the figure — goes through the check screen like everything else.
  2. When he answers, Record his answer: figure sent, he agrees, he disputes, or no answer yet. If he accepts a different number, type the number he accepts.
  3. Open his hisab in the CRM takes you to the full statement.

The bar at the top shows how much of the old book has been agreed and how much is still not taken up. That bar is the job.

A client with no mobile number

He cannot be rung, chased or sent his hisab, and the card says so in red. Accounts and the owner can fix it on the spot with Add his mobile. A mobile is ten digits; it will not take anything else.

When something is held on this phone

The banner above the tabs shows how much money is still waiting to go up, and it goes by itself when there is signal. Try now pushes it.

Nothing typed is ever lost. Even if the screen fails to draw, the app says so and tells you the payments held on that phone are still there and still going up.

Refresh will refuse while a sheet is open — it would clear what you have typed.
