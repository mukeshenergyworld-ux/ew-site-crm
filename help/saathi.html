EW Saathi - how to use

The partner app - points, offers and what to say at each stage. Show it to every new plumber and architect.

For the office: giving a partner the app

  1. Send him the EW Saathi link on WhatsApp.
  2. Give him his PIN on the phone - never on WhatsApp.
  3. Sit with him once: sign in, show Home and New, and ask him to send his PAN from Account.

Good to know

  • Nothing is ever deleted. Every delivery that earned points stays on his account, line by line.
  • Points on hold wait out the return window, then release by themselves.
  • 1 point is worth Rs 1. He is paid in rupees - by UPI or as a credit note.

Signing in

Who: Our plumbers, architects and other partners. The office gives each one his PIN.

EW Saathi signs in with the partner's mobile number and his PIN. The bar at the bottom: Home, New, Rate, Stage, Account.

Sign in

  1. Type the 10-digit mobile number and the PIN, then tap Sign in. Forgot it? Forgotten your PIN? - the office sets a new one.
  2. The ? at the top opens this guide at the screen you are on.

Home - your points

Who: The partner.

Ready to be paid now is what can be settled today; the bar shows how far to the next settlement. On hold is material still inside its return window - it releases by itself after the days shown. Below is every delivery that earned points.

Read it

  1. The big number is ready to pay. The four boxes: on hold, already paid, total so far, and your level.

Account - details and PAN

Who: The partner.

His details, how he is paid (UPI or credit note), his full account line by line, and how points are counted (1 point = Rs 1). Without a PAN more TDS is cut - Send PAN sends it on WhatsApp. Tell us if something looks wrong if a line is wrong.
