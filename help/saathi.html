The partner app — for plumbers and architects

Who this is for: the plumbers and architects who work with us — and the office, who has to explain it to them. The app itself is in Hinglish on purpose. The buttons below are quoted exactly as the partner sees them.

How a partner signs in

His mobile number and a PIN. First time, the PIN is the last four digits of his own number — the screen tells him so. He should change it with PIN badlein.

Only a man on the Associates sheet can get in. He cannot be added from the app; the office adds him.

The five tabs

TabWhat he sees
GharHis points — what he can draw now, what is still on hold, what he has already been paid, and how far off the next settlement is.
NayaWhat we have sent out: schemes, new products, offers.
RateHis own rate card — the points on each item, for his kind of partner.
StageConstruction stage by stage: what to say to the client and what to push at that stage.
HisaabHis profile, his full ledger, and how points turn into rupees.

What he does on an offer

  1. He opens Naya — or taps Dekhein on the card that tells him there is something new.
  2. He reads the offer.
  3. He taps Mujhe chahiye if he wants it, or Call karein to be rung back.

That is the whole of it. Somebody in the office then rings him.

From Stage he can tap a stage open and use Energy World se poochein, which opens WhatsApp to the office with the message ready.

Points are the headline, not rupees

The big number on his home screen is points. The rupee figure is shown underneath when the office has turned it on. Points hold for a set number of days before they can be drawn — the hold window is written on the screen, so he is never guessing.

If we owe him rupees and there is no PAN on file, he gets a nudge with PAN bhejein. If he thinks the ledger is wrong, Kuch galat lage to bataayein opens WhatsApp to us.

What the office should know

  • This app has no offline queue. Unlike the staff apps, if he has no signal it simply says so and he tries again. Nothing is lost because nothing was being written.
  • Anything we broadcast appears on his phone straight away. It is sent from the Console, and saving a broadcast does not send it — see that guide.
  • Nothing about a partner is ever deleted. Blocking a sign-in leaves his record, his points and his hisaab exactly as they were.
