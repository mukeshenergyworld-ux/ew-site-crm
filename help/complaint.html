Register a complaint

The form customers fill in — send them the link

This one is for customers, not for staff. Send them the link. There is no sign-in and no app to install — it opens in any browser and takes about a minute.

What the customer does

  1. Fills Your name and Mobile number, and picks his City if he likes.
  2. Taps what it is about: Product defect, Wrong / short supply, Delivery delay, Installation, Billing, Warranty or Other.
  3. Puts the Order / Challan / Invoice no. if he has it to hand.
  4. Writes what went wrong in Describe the issue.
  5. Sets Low, Normal or High if it is urgent.
  6. Taps Submit complaint.

He then gets a reference number on the screen, with a Copy button. Ask him for that number when he rings — it finds the complaint at once.

What it will not do, and why that matters

It never tells a customer his complaint was registered unless it truly was. If the send fails, the screen says Not sent yet in plain words, tells him it is saved on his phone and will go by itself, and gives him the office number to ring in the meantime. It used to show a false success; it does not any more.
  • It refuses to reload the page once he has started typing — that would clear it.
  • It will not send without a name, a ten-digit number, a category, and at least a line of description.
  • A failed complaint is queued on his phone and tried again by itself — never thrown away.

What the office should do

Complaints land on the sheet. Work them from the CRM. If a customer says he sent one and you cannot find it, ask whether his screen said registered with a reference number, or Not sent yet. Those two are very different and the screen is honest about which it was.
